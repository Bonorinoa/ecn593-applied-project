\section{Model and design details}\label{app:model}

\paragraph{The graph and the policy vector} The code draws
$A_{ij}\sim\mathrm{Bernoulli}(p)$ with $p=\EdgeRate/N$ for every ordered pair,
takes $A\lor A^\top$ and sets the diagonal to zero, so an edge appears with
probability $1-(1-p)^2\approx 2p$. The drawn graph has $\Triangles$ triangles over
$\ConnectedTriples$ connected triples, a global clustering coefficient of
$\GlobalClustering$ against $\GlobalClusteringTheory$ expected for a random graph
of this density. The graph is close to tree-like: two people with a common friend
are rarely friends themselves. In the policy vector $\dtwo$, ties in degree go to the lower
index: $\DegreeCutSeeded$ of the $\DegreeCutTotal$ people with degree
$\DegreeCut$ are seeded, and everyone above that degree is seeded. Given the graph,
$\dtwo$ is deterministic.

\paragraph{The update map is a contraction} The logistic function has slope at
most $1/4$, and the peer mean $(AY)_i/k_i$ is an average, so its sup norm is at
most that of $Y$. For two states under the same assignment and the same shocks,
one step of Equation~\eqref{eq:kernel} therefore shrinks their sup-norm distance by
a factor of at most $(\beta+\gamma)/4\le(\BoxBetaHi+\BoxGammaHi)/4<1$ over the
whole box. For a fixed assignment the process has a unique stationary regime and
forgets its starting state geometrically; after $T=\Horizon$ steps the starting
state matters by at most that factor to the power $T$, far less than the smallest
Monte Carlo standard error in Table~\ref{tab:recheck}. With a sum of neighbors'
outcomes in place of the average, the peer slope would grow with degree and the
bound would fail for parts of the box. The bound uses only the kernel, the box and
the horizon, all fixed before any run.

\paragraph{Random streams} Every random stream is seeded. Rep $r$ of every arm
shares one state stream, the starting state and all shocks, and each arm has its
own assignment stream, so contrasts between arms are paired. The image
replications use offsets $0$ to $\RepsEstimand-1$; the analysts' one panel is
offset $0$; the endpoint recheck, the Foil~2 gate and the repeated panels of
amendment A2 each use their own disjoint block (A2's starts at
$\TradeoffOffsetBase$). The pipeline is deterministic: a rerun reproduces every
digit.

\paragraph{History-matching waves} Figure~\ref{fig:waves} shows the waves. The
minimum implausibility in the three waves is $\WaveMinIOne$, $\WaveMinITwo$ and
$\WaveMinIThree$, and the median is $\WaveMedIOne$, $\WaveMedITwo$ and
$\WaveMedIThree$, against $\tau=\Tau$. Later waves concentrate on the kept region,
and the threshold does not move. The min-I point is the kept point with the
smallest $I$; the median-I point is the point at position
$\lfloor|\NROY|/2\rfloor$ when NROY is sorted by $I$.
