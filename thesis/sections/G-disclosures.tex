\section{Disclosures}\label{app:disclosures}

\paragraph{The pre-registration and its amendments} The design is
\texttt{docs/preregistration-v2.md}, frozen before any run of this version of the
project. Two amendments and one addendum were written later, and each says what
was already known when it was written. Amendment A1, the sensitivity menu of
Appendix~\ref{app:menu}, was written after the main results were known; it tests
no hypothesis. Amendment A2, the analysts A1 and A2 and the repeated-panel exercise
of Section~\ref{sec:a2results}, was written after the main and A1 results were
known, including A0's gap decomposition and Foil~2's calibration check, and before
any A2 code or number existed. Its five expectations were written then and are
reported as found. A later erratum to A2 changed wording only: the pilot realizes
the policy vector with a positive and negligible probability, where the first
wording had said that it never does. Addendum A2.1 was written after the A2
results and after the independent review of the A2 code, which had found the Young
bound with a probe at one point. It turns that probe into a committed, descriptive
split of the reported variance, with a fixed-noise redraw of the assignment
(Section~\ref{sec:a2results}). It changes no A2 estimator, interval, flag or
verdict, and it states no expectation.

\paragraph{Post hoc choices in the main design} The rescaling of A0's coefficient
by $K/N$ was adopted after the frozen numbers were inspected; its justification is
the aggregation identity under no interference. An earlier version of Foil~2 used a
first-order variance that dropped the covariance between strata; commit
\texttt{36ed3e6} replaced it with the Aronow--Samii variance, and no coverage
number from the earlier version is cited. The analysts' panel is also one of the
replications that define the targets (Section~\ref{sec:limits}).

\paragraph{Independent checks} The project's rule is that every statistical claim
is checked by a reviewer running on a model family different from the one that
produced it. The reviews are in \texttt{reviews/}. The wording of the A1 and A2 results in
Section~\ref{sec:a2results} follows the required wording of the review of
amendment A2 (\texttt{reviews/a2-code.md}).
