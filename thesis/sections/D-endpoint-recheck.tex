\section{The endpoint recheck}\label{app:recheck}

Each image endpoint is an extremum of $\ThetaSample$ noisy estimates, so the
standard error attached to the extremizing point is not a standard error for the
endpoint: it ignores the selection of the extremum. The code evaluates each
endpoint candidate again with $\RepsEstimand$ fresh replications on separate
streams (Table~\ref{tab:recheck}).

\begin{table}[ht]
\centering
\caption{Independent recheck of the endpoint candidates. SE is the pointwise Monte
Carlo standard error.}
\label{tab:recheck}
\begin{zebratabular}{lccc}
\toprule
\colhead{Endpoint} & \colhead{NROY index} & \colhead{Original (SE)} & \colhead{Fresh (SE)} \\
\midrule
$\Theta_{\hm}$ lower & $\RecheckHmLoIndex$ & $\RecheckHmLoPilot$ ($\RecheckHmLoPilotSE$) & $\RecheckHmLoFresh$ ($\RecheckHmLoFreshSE$) \\
$\Theta_{\hm}$ upper & $\RecheckHmHiIndex$ & $\RecheckHmHiPilot$ ($\RecheckHmHiPilotSE$) & $\RecheckHmHiFresh$ ($\RecheckHmHiFreshSE$) \\
$\Theta_{\expset}$ lower & $\RecheckExpLoIndex$ & $\RecheckExpLoPilot$ ($\RecheckExpLoPilotSE$) & $\RecheckExpLoFresh$ ($\RecheckExpLoFreshSE$) \\
$\Theta_{\expset}$ upper & $\RecheckExpHiIndex$ & $\RecheckExpHiPilot$ ($\RecheckExpHiPilotSE$) & $\RecheckExpHiFresh$ ($\RecheckExpHiFreshSE$) \\
\bottomrule
\end{zebratabular}
\end{table}

Fresh re-evaluation removes the reuse-of-noise bias for the selected candidates.
It is not simultaneous inference for the true extrema, and it says nothing about
extrema that the sample missed. Does the endpoint noise change any verdict? For
$\Theta_{\hm}$ the fresh values differ from the original ones only in the sixth
decimal. For $\Theta_{\expset}$, put the fresh values in place of the original
endpoints: the lower end of each calibrated Foil~2 interval is still above the
lower endpoint, the upper end is still below the upper endpoint, and the $\cstar$
interval still starts below the lower endpoint. None of the classifications
changes. This is a comparison of printed numbers, not a field of the results.
