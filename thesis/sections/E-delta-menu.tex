\section{The sensitivity menu for the level tolerance}\label{app:menu}

\emph{This analysis was decided after the main results were known.} Amendment A1
of the pre-registration was written once the main run had reported
$\Theta_{\hm}$ and the spread ratios, and once the allowance
$\sqrt{\tau\delta_1^2}$ had been set beside $\theta_1(\cstar)$. It tests no
hypothesis and is never used to choose $\delta$.

The menu sets the discrepancy of the two level moments to $\MenuDeltaMainLevel$
(Main), $\MenuDeltaHalfLevel$ (Half) or $\MenuDeltaQuarterLevel$ (Quarter). The two
correlation moments keep $\delta=\DeltaTwo$. For each entry the code reruns the
waves with the same seed, NROY, the $\ThetaSample$-point image sample and the
spread ratios; $\tau$, $C$, the targets and every seed are unchanged. The Main
entry reproduces the main run exactly, and the script stops with an error if it
does not. The analysts are not rerun at the calibrator's points, because those
points move with $\delta$; the field experimenter's intervals at $\cstar$ do not
depend on $\delta$, so they are only classified against each entry's images.

The tolerance matters most for $\kappa$ and $\alpha$. The kept set shrinks as
$\delta_1$ falls, and at Quarter both images are narrower than at Main. The spread
ratio for $\kappa$ falls to $\MenuDeltaQuarterSpreadRatioKappa$ and the one for
$\alpha$ to $\MenuDeltaQuarterSpreadRatioAlpha$, which fits the reading that the
level allowance is what leaves $\kappa$ open. The ratio for $\gamma$ stays near one
($\MenuDeltaQuarterSpreadRatioGamma$ at Quarter), but the menu leaves the
correlation tolerance alone, so it says nothing new about $\gamma$. The entries are
not nested: the upper end of both images at Half sits above the one at Main,
because each image is the range over a sample from a different finite NROY set,
with the waves redrawn. We do not read small moves of an endpoint as a trend. The
clear change is at Quarter, where the lower end of $\Theta_{\expset}$ is above zero
($\MenuDeltaQuarterThetaExpLo$); under Main and Half the sign of the spillover is
open. No entry returns F-EMPTY or F-SINGLETON. The verdicts at $\cstar$ do not
move, and a smaller $\delta_1$ does not give Foil~2 a verdict at $\cstar$ even at
Quarter.

This menu is the reporting rule of \citet{tamer2010} applied to $\delta$: a set
under each of several assumptions, instead of one answer. In this planted exercise
the targets are the simulator's own output, so the smaller tolerances are not an
unfounded claim here. With external targets they would have to be defended. We
adopt none of the entries; the paper's results stay at Main.
